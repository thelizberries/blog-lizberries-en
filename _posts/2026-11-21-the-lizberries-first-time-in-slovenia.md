# Lizberries in Slovenia: Two Memorable Gigs

Slovenia will forever be synonymous with one of the most beautiful tours we’ve ever done. 

<!--more-->


A truly charming country, with gentle, delicate landscapes that, in some places, remind one of an even more charming Austria. And then there are the people: talkative, welcoming, warm, and, above all… absolutely passionate about **The Cranberries**!

That’s right, you heard correctly: at both gigs, we were met by a huge crowd that knew and sang along to every single Cranberries song. But let’s take it one step at a time, because this trip deserves to be told from the very beginning.

## Friday, November 13: Destination Murska Sobota!

We left Milan at 1:00 p.m. The trip went by almost without stops: just five minutes to sample some **Bora Nera** at the rest stop just past Trieste, and then off we went toward Slovenia. In total, seven hours by car.

Of course, Stefano talked for 6 hours and 50 minutes. The only moment of silence? When he spotted a sign that said “Follow Me.” But even that didn’t last long!

At 8:30 p.m., we finally arrive in **Murska Sobota**, practically a stone’s throw from the borders with Austria and Hungary. The limited time we had didn’t allow us to fully appreciate the beauty of the place and the venue hosting us, but one glance was enough to realize we’d ended up in a special, famous, and much-loved spot.

It’s called **Bunker**, and it’s a pub in perfect post-apocalyptic style, with incredibly original decor and atmosphere. It’s not every day you get to play with two soldiers holding machine guns right behind you! Luckily, they were fake…

Time flies: we can barely stop for a moment, but that doesn’t stop us from immediately sensing the extraordinary warmth of **Igor**, the owner (who we only discover at the end is also the guest of honor today!), his daughter **Iva**, and the two talented sound engineers.

Just enough time to set everything up, grab a quick bite, and we’re on stage.

## At the Bunker: An Audience That Sings Every Song

And what a gig!

Two wonderful hours, with the venue packed—no, overflowing. The audience is practically glued to the small stage and sings at the top of their lungs from start to finish. There are even people who settle in on the stairs and stay there for the entire gig, just so they don’t miss a single song.

The excitement is through the roof. At the end, we received tons of compliments and even a small bouquet of flowers. Because let’s face it: Slovenians and Austrians really know how to be chivalrous!

But the pampering didn’t end there. So we wouldn’t have to face the night alone after such an intense evening, **they booked us two rooms** at the hotel right next to the venue: the **Hotel Belmur**. What a surprise!

The hotel is extraordinary, comfortable, with everything we need—and even a little more than we expected. Finally, we can rest, relax, and recharge our batteries.

And then comes breakfast…

## Slovenian breakfast: a special treat

Here we need to give a special shout-out to **Branko**, who practically never stops laying the table with all kinds of delicacies.

Among the many treats is the classic Slovenian cake, with its irresistible blueberry topping. A breakfast that, on its own, would be worth the trip!

## Saturday, November 14: Destination Ljubljana!

Refreshed in body and soul, on Saturday, November 14, we set off for **Ljubljana**, the Slovenian capital.

The day is simply gorgeous: a deep blue sky, vibrant fall colors, and warm air that almost feels like a gift. We couldn’t have asked for anything better!

We even find a parking spot right in the city center on our first try. At that point, we start looking at each other with a certain amount of suspicion: after so much good luck, when will the day’s bad luck strike to even things out?

But no. No bad luck, no unexpected events: it’s simply going to be a perfect day, just like Lou Reed sang.

We start by heading up toward the castle, tackling a narrow path that winds its way through the trees and, with all those autumn leaves, is definitely slippery. But the effort is more than rewarded by the view of Ljubljana from above: an enchanting, cozy, colorful city surrounded by a landscape that looks like something out of a postcard.

Then we head down toward the city center, where markets, little shops, and many small wonders await us.

## Bear Goulash and Shopping Among the Dragons

At some point, of course, we get hungry. And here we make a decidedly unusual culinary discovery: a finger-food spot serving Slovenian specialties, including… bear goulash!

Yes, you read that right: **BEAR**.

We feel a little sorry for the bear, but we have to admit it was delicious!

Refreshed, we treat ourselves to a bit of shopping, especially at a renowned dragon shop. After all, everyone knows that Ljubljana is the city of dragons—the undisputed symbol of the capital and the stars of the famous **Dragon Bridge**. How could we leave without taking a few home with us?

But 4:00 p.m. is approaching, the sun is starting to set, and reality is setting in again: alas—but also hooray—we have to go to work!

## At the Orto Bar: Another Unforgettable gig

The **Orto Bar** awaits us, with its incredibly friendly staff. The bouncer is a constant source of jokes, the sound engineers are fantastic, and the stage is exactly the kind we like: nice and big, just like the venue itself—spacious and long.

The question naturally arises: will we be able to fill the whole place?

Of course we will!

This evening turns out to be wonderful as well. So many people came to listen to the music of **The Cranberries**, and among them are stories that will stay in our hearts.

There’s a guy who came all the way from Austria—after a six-hour trip—with a beautiful white rose for us. And there were also those who, after attending the previous night’s gig at the Bunker, decided to repeat the experience, hopping in the car with their whole family and driving another two hours just to hear us play again.

How can you not be moved by so much passion?

Here, too, the two-hour gig flies by. The audience is extraordinary—they sing along, join in, and give us incredible energy. There are moments that can’t be easily described, because you have to experience them: the looks in their eyes, the voices blending with ours, the enthusiasm, and that wonderful feeling of all being—at least for one evening—part of the same big family.

We step off the stage with our hearts full and the knowledge that we’ve experienced something truly special. Another gig, another wonderful audience, more memories destined to last.

## Sunday, November 15: A “See You Later,” Not a “Goodbye”

The next morning we wake up tired but incredibly happy.

Our little Slovenian dream is over, and unfortunately, it’s time to think about the trip home. But first, of course, we can’t pass up one last taste of the local cuisine.

We treat ourselves to some delicious *palačinke* with gianduia and a hot cappuccino. A fitting conclusion, especially considering that the day began in a completely different way from the previous one: gray skies, rain, and **thick clouds** that seem to be gently escorting us home.

And so we say goodbye to Slovenia.

We’re taking home wonderful landscapes, new flavors, lots of warmth, a welcome we’ll hardly ever forget, and two incredible gigs, full of music, smiles, and emotions.

But above all, we’re taking one certainty with us: around here, **The Cranberries** are truly beloved!

**Goodbye, Slovenia—see you soon.** Because, let’s not beat around the bush, we’ve left a little piece of our hearts with you. ❤️
