We just got back from an exciting evening at the **Hard Rock Cafe in Milan**, which took place on Saturday, October 3, 2026. Playing in your own city 

<!--more-->


is always a unique thrill, but doing it in the heart of Milan, on such an iconic stage, is a true honor.

The venue was sold out, bursting with energy and filled with people who came just for us. Among the many gestures of affection, we were especially touched by a couple who traveled over 500 km by train just to hear us play.

## A Sold-Out Evening in Milan

Faced with such a warm and engaged audience, we can only say **thank you**.

Lately, we’ve been receiving so much kindness that it feels like we’re taking home much more than we’re able to give from the stage.

Playing in **Milan**, our hometown, makes everything even more special.

But being able to share our music with a packed house and with people who came specifically to hear us is something that moves us deeply.

## The Music of The Cranberries and the Magic of Live Performance

Not even the flu could stop **Kry**’s voice!

Every time we sing the songs of the **Cranberries**, something special happens: we enter a different dimension and the energy transforms.

Whether it’s the timeless power of their music or Dolores watching over us from above, that same incredible magic ignited again last night.

It’s hard to explain exactly what happens during these moments.

We can only experience them.

And continue to bring the Cranberries’ music to the stage with all the emotion that binds us to these songs.

## Thank you, Milan!

Milan filled us with joy, and we hope we gave back every single vibration to you.

To everyone who came to hear us, to those who traveled from far away, and to those who continue to support us:

**thank you from the bottom of our hearts.** ❤️

🍀 **SPOILER:** Mark your calendars!

We’re returning to the **Hard Rock Cafe in Milan on March 17** to celebrate **St. Patrick’s Day** together!

We look forward to seeing you for another evening of music, The Cranberries, and, of course, plenty of magic.
